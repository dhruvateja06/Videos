When it's just you

For one user, a system is almost nothing.

Your phone asks. One computer answers. It looks up your data. Done.
Now make it a million

Point the whole city at that one computer.

  • It runs out of memory, then it stops answering.Now nobody's order goes through — not even yours.
So, the actual definition

System design is deciding
what boxes you need — and how they connect —

so it stays fast and standing as users grow.

No magic. Just boxes and arrows — and the trade-offs of which box goes where.
The building blocks

Add a box for each job. This is the whole season on one screen.

It's not just "does it work"

Every choice is judged by two numbers.

Latency
How long one request takes.
You feel this directly — the wait between tap and answer. Measured in milliseconds.
Throughput
How many you handle per second.
The crowd feels this — can a million be served at once without a queue forming.
Good system design keeps both healthy — fast for one, and fast for a million.
