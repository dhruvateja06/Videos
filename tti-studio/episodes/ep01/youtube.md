## Full description
You open your favourite app at 8pm. At the same second, half your city does too — and it just works. No crash, no spinning wheel. How is that even possible?

That's **system design**, and you don't need a CS degree to get it. In episode 1 of The Tech Intern — System Design, we learn the whole foundation by designing a "baby Instagram" from scratch: post a photo, see your friends' feed.

You'll walk away understanding:
- The 3 pieces inside almost every app: client, server, database
- What a request and a response actually are (the librarian analogy)
- The request's journey — the one path hiding inside every app you use
- The 4 steps engineers follow every time: requirements, high-level design, core components, scale
- HLD vs LLD, in plain English
- How to scale from 10 users to 10 million (and why you shouldn't build for 10 million on day one)
- How Disney+ Hotstar held 59 million concurrent viewers during the 2023 World Cup final — using the exact same ideas

No jargon left unexplained. Indian, everyday examples first.

▶ Next episode: how your phone actually *finds* the server out of the entire internet — DNS & HTTP.
