the 8pm swiggy rush

three very different problems · same friday night

"The app is slow" means three different things.

latency · how long one round trip takes

Latency (how long one order takes, door to door)

One person. One tap. 65 milliseconds later — the screen updates.
what makes that 65ms become 8,000ms?

Three things push latency up.

Distance is physics. Processing is code. The database is usually the culprit.
throughput · orders per second

Throughput (how many orders per second)

The pipe didn't get faster. We added more pipes. That's throughput.
the kitchen · season metaphor, introduced here

The kitchen can make 50 rotis an hour. Hungry or not.

bandwidth · the width of the pipe

Bandwidth is how wide the pipe is.

Latency = how fast. Throughput = how many. Bandwidth = how much at once.
the one thing people mix up · 8pm vs 3am

High throughput. High latency. Both at once. They're independent.

IRCTC Tatkal at 10am: thousands queuing, each waiting 20 seconds. High load, terrible latency.
the two numbers every engineer quotes

p50 · p99 · qps — what they actually mean.

p50 is the middle. p99 is the tail. qps is the speed. Three numbers, full picture.
the tail catches what the average hides

The average is lying to you.

If you only watch the average, the tail is invisible. p99 is how you see it.
back to our friday night · now you understand it

When demand > throughput, a queue forms.

The kitchen didn't get slower. There are just more orders than it can cook.
bandwidth at scale · hotstar ipl 2023

IPL Final. 32 million streams. Simultaneously.

You can't serve 128 Tbps from one building. That's why the CDN exists.
latency vs throughput · the engineer's decision

You can trade latency for throughput. Engineers choose.

Real-time money = latency first. Batch analytics = throughput first. No universal winner.
